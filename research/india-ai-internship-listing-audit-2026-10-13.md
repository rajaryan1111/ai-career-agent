## Eligibility and quality implications

1. **Remote is not a geography guarantee.** Sprinto's posting says Bengaluru and Remote, but the inspected page did not explicitly define candidate-country eligibility. Preserve eligibility as unknown until confirmed; do not silently label it worldwide.
2. **Internship does not mean suitable for every student.** Meraki's indexed description includes strong institution/academic signals and a public GitHub requirement. A generic “currently studying” filter would overstate eligibility.
3. **Stale but reachable is not open.** Sarvam's listing remains a useful source lead and ML-data-pipeline example, but its indexed page is months old. Do not recommend it as active without a fresh canonical check.
4. **ATS shell limitations must be visible.** Ashby pages can return a JavaScript-only shell to a text retrieval method. That is “details not retrievable by this method,” not proof that the job is missing or unavailable. Use an approved, bounded alternative or mark the fields unknown; do not circumvent access controls.
5. **Application form visibility does not authorize automated submission.** The current source audit continues to evaluate discovery and application submission separately.
